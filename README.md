🛒 Flipkart Big Billion Day Insights 2025 Dashboard

##📊 Overview

The **Flipkart Big Billion Day Insights 2025** dashboard provides a comprehensive visual analysis of sales performance, customer engagement, and purchasing behavior during one of India’s largest shopping events — the **Big Billion Days Sale**.

This dashboard showcases key performance metrics such as **total sales amount**, **number of buyers**, **top-performing products**, **cities and states by revenue**, **payment preferences**, and **customer feedback by category**.

## 📅 Monthly Insights

* **Month: September** — Covers days 23 to 30.
* **Month: October** — Covers days 1 and 2.
  This shows the entire **Big Billion Days event window**, capturing late September through early October.

3. **Delhi** – Third top-performing state

### 🏘️ Top 5 Cities by Sales Amount

* **Ahmedabad**
* **Surat**

## ⚙️ Features of the Dashboard

* 📈 **Interactive Data Visualization:** Displays key Flipkart Big Billion Day insights with colorful bar, pie, and donut charts.
* 🗺️ **Regional Analysis:** State and city-level breakdown of sales amount.
* 🧾 **Product Analysis:** Shows top searched products and best-selling categories.
* 💰 **Payment Insights:** Visual distribution of customer payment methods.
* 💬 **Customer Feedback Trends:** Insights on feedback volume per category.
* 📆 **Monthly Filter:** Allows tracking by event days (September–October).
* 🧠 **Data-Driven Insights:** Supports marketing, sales, and operational decisions.
